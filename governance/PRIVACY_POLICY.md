# EduMind AI Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0.0

## Data We Collect

- Account information (email, name, role)
- Usage logs (hashed, not raw content)
- Session metadata (duration, topics, no audio stored)

## Data We Don't Store

- Raw student answers (processed then discarded)
- Voice recordings (transcribed and deleted)
- PDF documents (processed then deleted)
- Credit card or payment information

## Data Retention

- Audit logs: 90 days
- User accounts: Until deletion requested
- Session metadata: 30 days
- Uploaded files: Deleted after processing

## Your Rights

- Request data deletion: DELETE /api/v1/user/data
- Export your data: GET /api/v1/user/export
- Opt out of analytics: Settings > Privacy
